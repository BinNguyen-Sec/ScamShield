# Contributing to ScamShield

Thank you for your interest in ScamShield. The project is in Research & Discovery, so useful contributions include research, documentation, source evaluation, threat modeling, and carefully scoped prototypes.

## Before Contributing

- Read the project [README](README.md) and [Privacy Principles](docs/privacy-principles.md).
- Check existing issues and discussions before starting substantial work.
- Explain the problem, evidence, and expected impact of a proposed change.

## Research Contributions

When adding a statistic, report, or external dataset, include:
- Publisher and source URL.
- Publication date and access date.
- Geographic and population scope.
- Methodology, where available.
- Important caveats and limitations.
- License or terms of use, where relevant.

Do not present exploratory survey findings as nationally representative unless the sampling design supports that conclusion.

## Security and Privacy

- Never submit passwords, OTPs, private messages, or personal records.
- Do not include raw survey responses or identifiable participant information.
- Do not test systems without authorization.
- Do not submit live malware, exploit payloads, or harmful links as ordinary examples.
- Clearly label unverified indicators and supporting evidence.

## Pull Requests

Keep changes focused. Include a short description, relevant sources, and tests or validation steps where applicable. Be transparent about assumptions and known limitations.

## Current Project Direction

The initial focus is URL/domain threat intelligence and local browser protection. Large feature expansions should be supported by research and discussed before implementation.
