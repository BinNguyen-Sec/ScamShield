# Research

This directory contains the evidence base for ScamShield's product decisions.

## Research Areas

- [Market research](market-research/): Vietnam scam landscape, public statistics, and policy initiatives.
- [User research](user-research/): survey design, aggregated findings, and interview insights.
- [Competitor analysis](competitor-analysis/): existing tools, capabilities, limitations, and privacy approaches.

## Evidence Standard

For each material claim, record the publisher, title, publication date, URL, scope, methodology, access date, and caveats. Separate official case statistics, survey estimates, and independent research; they are not directly interchangeable.

Do not publish identifiable survey responses or private participant information.
