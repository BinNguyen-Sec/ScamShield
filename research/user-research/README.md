# User Research

This directory stores research plans and aggregated findings about online habits, scam exposure, protective behaviors, and privacy expectations.

## Principles

- Participation should be voluntary and the purpose should be clear.
- Avoid collecting sensitive personal information.
- Do not request passwords, OTPs, financial-account details, or private message content.
- Keep raw responses access-controlled and out of the public repository.
- Publish only aggregated or appropriately anonymized results.
- Describe recruitment and sampling limitations.

Survey results are exploratory unless a representative sampling method is used.
