# Product Vision

## Purpose

ScamShield explores an accessible, privacy-first way to help people recognize risky online destinations, beginning with URLs and domains.

## Problem Hypothesis

People encounter suspicious links across messaging, social media, email, search, and online marketplaces. Protective information and reporting mechanisms may be fragmented, and users may lack timely, understandable context when deciding whether to open a link.

This is a hypothesis to investigate through user research and public evidence, not a conclusion assumed in advance.

## Intended Users

- Everyday internet users with different levels of technical literacy.
- People who frequently use online banking, shopping, recruitment, and digital services.
- Security researchers and volunteers who can help assess threat indicators.

## Product Principles

1. Privacy by design and data minimization.
2. Evidence-based threat classification.
3. Clear explanations and calibrated warnings.
4. Local checks where practical.
5. Accessible, low-friction user experience.
6. Honest communication of coverage and limitations.

## Initial Product Hypothesis

A browser extension that checks destinations against a periodically updated local threat-intelligence dataset may help users avoid known malicious or fraudulent websites without routinely transmitting browsing activity to a central service.

## Success Measures to Validate

- Detection precision and false-positive rate on a documented test set.
- Feed freshness and update reliability.
- Extension performance and permission footprint.
- User comprehension of warning messages.
- User trust and willingness to install the extension.
- Accessibility across different levels of digital literacy.

Metrics and thresholds will be defined after baseline research.
